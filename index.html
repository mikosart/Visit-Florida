COPY BANK

These copy examples serve as guidance for the brand tone of voice in action. Be mindful of character limits set by media placements. Shorter limits should include harder-hitting points, while longer limits can lean more toward emotional storytelling.

Traditional and digital media placements should overtly state the message we're trying to convey, leaving no questions to the viewer, only a curiosity to learn more and a desire to book a trip.

Social copy should be more conversational and relevant to trending topics within the platform. This is a space to ask open ended questions to foster engagement and conversation.
