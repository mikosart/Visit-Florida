Floridays in Motion — Video Options

Below are three options for displaying videos on the toolkit. Click on the thumbnails in Options 2 and 3 to see how they work.

Option 1

Direct YouTube Embed

The YouTube player loads directly on the page. Visitors just press play.

Pros

  • Simplest — no extra click needed
  • Familiar YouTube controls

Cons

  • Slower page load
  • YouTube branding visible
About the thumbnail No custom thumbnail possible. The preview is controlled entirely by YouTube.
Option 2

Thumbnail + Lightbox Modal

Displays a thumbnail with a play button. Clicking opens a centered overlay with the video.

A) YouTube default thumbnail

B) Your custom image

Pros

  • Fast page load
  • On-brand thumbnails
  • Focused video overlay

Cons

  • Extra click required
  • Modal can feel odd on mobile
About the thumbnail A) Auto-updates from YouTube.
B) Your own branded image — different per language (EN/ES/PT).
Option 3 — Recommended

Thumbnail + Lazy-Load (Hybrid)

Same thumbnails as Option 2, but clicking replaces the image in-place with the video. No popup.

A) YouTube default thumbnail

B) Your custom image

Pros

  • Fast page load
  • On-brand thumbnails
  • Video plays in-place — smooth & native feel
  • Best balance of performance & UX

Cons

  • Extra click required
  • Thumbnail gone until page refresh
About the thumbnail A) Auto-updates from YouTube.
B) Your own branded image — different per language (EN/ES/PT).
