What This Option Does

The YouTube video player is loaded and visible as soon as the page opens. A visitor just clicks the play button on the video to start watching it right there on the page.

Pros

  • Simplest approach — visitors instantly see that video content is available
  • Familiar YouTube player controls (captions, quality, fullscreen)
  • No extra clicks needed; just press play

Cons

  • Slower page load — YouTube iframe loads resources even if nobody watches
  • Less visual control — the YouTube player may not match the site's design
  • YouTube branding and suggested videos may appear
About the thumbnail This option does NOT use a custom thumbnail. The preview image you see before pressing play is controlled entirely by YouTube. Whatever thumbnail is set on the YouTube video is what will appear here. There is no way to customize it from the website side.
