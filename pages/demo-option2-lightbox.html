A) Default YouTube Thumbnail

This uses the thumbnail that is set on the YouTube video. If the video owner changes the thumbnail on YouTube, it automatically updates here too.

B) Custom Thumbnail (your current image)

This uses your own custom image as the thumbnail. You have full control over the design — it won't change even if the YouTube thumbnail changes. This is ideal for branded images with logos or text in different languages.

What This Option Does

Visitors see a thumbnail image that looks like part of the page design. A play button overlay signals that video is available. When they click, a dark overlay appears with the video front and center. They can close it with the × button or by clicking outside.

Pros

  • Fast page load — no YouTube resources until the visitor actually clicks
  • Beautiful, on-brand thumbnails that match the site design perfectly
  • Video opens in a focused, distraction-free overlay
  • The rest of the page stays in place; closing the modal returns to where they were

Cons

  • Requires an extra click before the video plays
  • Overlay/modal can feel unexpected on some mobile devices
About the thumbnail You can choose either option:
A) YouTube default: The thumbnail updates automatically if it's changed on YouTube. No maintenance needed, but you can't control the design.
B) Custom image: You use your own branded image (like the current one). You have full design control and can have different images per language (EN/ES/PT). It won't change unless you update it manually.
